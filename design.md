# Lumi Beauty design system

## Direction

Lumi Beauty uses a quiet luxury editorial commerce language: warm ivory surfaces,
ink typography, restrained rose and plum accents, generous whitespace, large product
imagery, soft corners, and subtle elevation. Interaction must remain obvious without
turning every content block into a card.

## Tokens

- Color: ivory `#FCF9F7`, paper `#FFFFFF`, ink `#241B20`, muted ink `#6F6268`, rose
  `#B5486D`, pale rose `#F7E9EE`, plum `#65354C`, success `#28785A`, warning `#9A641D`.
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80.
- Radius: 10 controls, 16 cards, 24 feature surfaces, full only for chips and icon buttons.
- Motion: 160ms feedback, 240ms component transitions, 320ms section entrance; ease-out.
- Content width: 1280px standard, 1440px wide commerce grids.
- Breakpoints: 600 mobile, 900 tablet/navigation, 1200 desktop, 1440 large.

## Component character

Navigation is calm and typographic with a short active underline. Cards use either a
soft shadow or a hairline border, never both. Product imagery is the visual lead.
Buttons use sentence case and name a real action. Empty/error/loading states always
explain the recovery path.
